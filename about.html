About Stackly

A workplace built by people who got tired of lonely desks.

Stackly Cowork started in 2021 with 20 desks and one big table. Today it is the busiest working community in Salem.

Open since 2021
Our story

Started with one table

Our founders were freelancers working from cafés and spare rooms, missing the energy of a team. They rented a floor in MMR Complex, bought one long table and invited ten friends.

That table is still in the middle of the floor. Around it we added quiet zones, meeting rooms, an event hall and a rooftop garden, always asking members what to build next.

Good work happens when people feel safe to ask for help, share what they know, and show up on a bad day too.

Inside Stackly

One building, five ways to work

Every floor is planned around how people actually spend their day, from a ten minute call to a full week of heads-down building.

  • Open floor with hot desks, standing benches and the long founding table.
  • Quiet zone with no calls, no chatter and strict phone-free desks.
  • Private offices for 2 to 12 people, lockable and fully furnished.
  • Meeting rooms with screens, whiteboard walls and video-call kits.
  • Event hall and rooftop garden for workshops, demos and Friday chai.

Good to know

Questions people ask before visiting

Can I try Stackly before I commit?

Yes. Book a free tour and we will give you a day pass so you can work from the floor and meet a few members.

Are there long contracts?

No. Hot desks are monthly, and you can upgrade, downgrade or pause your plan without penalties.

Is the space open on weekends?

Members have keycard access every day of the week. Staffed hours are Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 9 pm.

Can I host a client meeting or an event?

Meeting rooms can be booked by the hour, and the event hall seats up to 80 guests with AV and catering support.

Do you offer a business address for registration?

Yes. Dedicated desk and private office plans include a registered address and mail handling.

Meet the team in person.

Drop in any weekday. Chai is on us.
